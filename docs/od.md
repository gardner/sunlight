## Short public description for Open Data Ltd (Charity)

**Open Data Limited is a non-profit data activism organisation working to make important data in New Zealand open, free, searchable, and reusable.**

We identify public-interest data that is closed, fragmented, hard to find, locked in poor formats, or unavailable to the people who need it. Then we build practical projects, tools, archives, requests, and public-interest infrastructure to make that information easier to access and use.

Our work supports journalism, research, civic technology, public understanding, accountability, and better decision-making.

## Slightly longer version

**Open Data Limited works to open up New Zealand’s public-interest data.**

Too much useful information is technically public but practically inaccessible. It may be scattered across websites, buried in PDFs, locked in inboxes, missing from APIs, inconsistently published, or difficult to search and reuse.

Open Data Limited identifies these closed-data problems and works to fix them. We collect and preserve public-interest information, build searchable archives and tools, advocate for better data access, and host projects that make official information more useful to the public.

Our first major project is **Sunlight**, which collects and preserves official information response material from public authorities so it can be reviewed, searched, and reused responsibly.

## Plain-English mission statement

**Our mission is to make public-interest data open, free, available, searchable, and useful.**

We believe important information should not disappear into private inboxes, broken links, inaccessible formats, or systems only specialists can use.

Open Data Limited builds practical infrastructure that helps people find, understand, verify, and reuse information that matters.

## What “data activism” means here

For Open Data Limited, **data activism** means practical work to improve access to information.

It means we:

* identify where useful data is closed, missing, fragmented, or unusable;
* ask for information that should be easier to access;
* preserve public-interest records before they disappear;
* turn scattered files into searchable archives;
* build tools that help people use public information;
* advocate for open, reusable, well-documented data;
* support responsible publication where privacy, safety, and context matter.

This is not activism in the party-political sense. It is civic, practical, non-partisan work focused on public access to information.

## What Open Data Limited does

### We identify closed data

We look for public-interest information that is unavailable, hard to find, hard to search, or hard to reuse.

Closed data can include information that is:

* not published at all;
* only available through one-off requests;
* scattered across different websites;
* buried in PDFs or spreadsheets;
* missing useful metadata;
* difficult to compare over time;
* technically public but practically inaccessible.

### We open pathways to access

We use lawful, practical methods to improve access to information. That may include official information requests, public records research, agency engagement, data collection, scraping public sources where appropriate, and building better interfaces for existing public material.

### We preserve information

Public information is only useful if it can still be found later. We preserve records, files, metadata, source context, and provenance so information remains useful over time.

### We build tools and archives

We create public-interest infrastructure such as searchable archives, data tools, APIs, indexes, explainers, and civic technology projects.

### We host public-interest projects

Open Data Limited is the home for projects that improve access to information. **Sunlight** is the first major example: a project focused on collecting and preserving official information disclosure material. 

### We advocate for better data practices

We support open, reusable, well-documented data. That includes better publication practices, clearer metadata, open formats, responsible licensing, and systems that make information easier for the public to use.

## Suggested “About” copy

**About Open Data Limited**

Open Data Limited exists to make important information easier to access, understand, and reuse.

We work on public-interest data: information that helps people understand public decisions, public systems, public money, public services, and public power.

Much of this information is technically available but practically difficult to use. It may be scattered across agency websites, locked in one-off email responses, published in poor formats, missing from public indexes, or unavailable without specialist knowledge.

We call this **closed data**.

Our work is to identify those gaps and reduce them. We collect and preserve information, build tools and archives, support open publication, and host projects that make data more useful for the public.

Open Data Limited is independent, non-partisan, and strictly non-profit.

## Suggested principles

### Open by default

Public-interest data should be freely available where lawful, responsible, and practical.

### Usable, not just available

Publishing data is not enough. Information should be searchable, understandable, well-documented, and reusable.

### Provenance matters

People should be able to see where information came from and how it has been handled.

### Public benefit first

Our work supports journalism, research, civic technology, public understanding, accountability, and democratic participation.

### Careful handling

Not everything should be published automatically. Privacy, safety, security, legal obligations, and context matter.

### Independent and non-partisan

Open Data Limited is not a government service, political party, or campaign organisation.
