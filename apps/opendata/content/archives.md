# Archives

The Sunlight Project manages several long-term structural data archives.

## Active Archives

- **Sunlight**: Our foundational archive designed to systematically preserve and index materials released under the Official Information Act (OIA).

## Data Access

We believe in making public-interest data open, free, searchable, and reusable. We process data systematically and provide clear provenance documentation. Raw data and bulk exports will be made available through our robust, documented APIs as they become available.
