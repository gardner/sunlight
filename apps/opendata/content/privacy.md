# Privacy Policy

**The Sunlight Project** respects your privacy. 

As an organization dedicated to public-interest data infrastructure, we prioritize structural clarity and minimal data collection.

## What we collect

We do not use tracking cookies, analytics pixels, or invasive surveillance scripts on this website. Our server logs may temporarily collect standard connection metadata (such as IP addresses and user agents) strictly for security, rate-limiting, and diagnostic purposes.

## How we use data

If you contact us via email, your correspondence is used solely for the purpose of communicating with you and will not be shared with third parties without your consent, except where required by law.

## Contacting the Privacy Officer

If you have questions about our privacy practices, you can contact our Privacy Officer at [privacy@opendata.org.nz](mailto:privacy@opendata.org.nz).
